Rollywood Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Overview

Rollywood (“the app”) is a mobile game for Android developed by Dương Hiếu (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is collected when you use the app, how it is used, and the choices you have.

In short: we do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to deliver those ads, as described below.

Information stored only on your device

Your game progress — scores, your personal Top 5 leaderboard, coins, unlocked marble skins and settings — is saved locally on your device. It is never sent to us. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Motion sensors

The app reads your device's motion sensors (accelerometer / gravity sensor) so you can steer the marble by tilting your phone. Sensor readings are processed on your device in real time and are never stored or transmitted.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads provided by Google AdMob (banner, full-screen and optional rewarded ads). To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google:

DataExamplesUsed for
IP addressMay be used to estimate your general (approximate) locationAdvertising, fraud prevention
Device identifiersAndroid advertising ID, app set IDAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
App interactionsApp launches, taps, ad viewsAdvertising, analytics
DiagnosticsApp launch time, hang rate, energy usageAnalytics, fraud prevention

This data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Watching a rewarded ad is always optional; it only gives an in-game reward (for example, continuing a run or extra coins).

Your choices

  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch we ask for your consent using Google's consent tool before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy option in the app's main menu.
  • Delete your game data: clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and shared with Google as described above, the app does not share any information with third parties.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Data retention and security

We do not operate servers that store information about you. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies. We keep the app and its third-party components up to date to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
